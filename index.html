About RM202

A room, a model, and a circle of artists who draw the body as it is.

RM202 runs live nude painting and drawing sessions for artists of every level. We work from professional models in a safe, respectful space where the body is celebrated, not judged.

We're a community centred on LGBTQIA+ artists, especially gay men, and open to everyone who comes to make art with care. Sessions began in Quezon City and are open to artists from anywhere.

  • Consent first. No photos of models without permission.
  • All levels. Bring a pencil or a full kit.
  • 18+ only. Valid ID may be asked at the door.
